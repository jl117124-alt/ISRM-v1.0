\# Domain Constraints and Known Limits

ISRM v1.0 operates under the following constraints:

1\. Requires consistent subnational data.  
2\. Sensitive to normalization domain selection.  
3\. Not portable to metro-level datasets lacking IRD proxies.  
4\. Not suited for event-level forecasting.  
5\. Not causal.  
6\. Dependent on structural variance within domain.  
7\. Not validated outside federated governance systems.

Violation of these constraints invalidates inference.
