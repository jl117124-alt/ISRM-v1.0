\# Ethical Use Statement

ISRM v1.0 is a structural diagnostic instrument.

It does not:  
\- Authorize coercion  
\- Justify policy enforcement  
\- Determine legitimacy  
\- Rank human worth

It should not be used to:  
\- Target specific populations  
\- Justify punitive expansion  
\- Support deterministic narratives

Transparency is the primary safeguard.

The framework is open so it cannot be monopolized.
