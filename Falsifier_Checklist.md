\# ISRM v1.0 Falsifier Checklist

Any one failure below invalidates core claims:

1\. Cohen’s d \< 1.0 in U.S. 2010–2024 regime separation.  
2\. IRD removal produces equal or greater regime separation.  
3\. Functional additive model performs equivalently to multiplicative model.  
4\. Frozen out-of-sample separation collapses below d \= 0.8.  
5\. Monte Carlo ±15% noise collapses d below 0.8.  
6\. Cross-domain replication yields sign inversion.

If any threshold is met, the model requires revision.
